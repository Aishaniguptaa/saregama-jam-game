# Concept

Your game, in your words. Claude builds from this file, so what you write here steers everything.

## Pitch

*In one or two sentences, what is the game?*

> You are an Indian dancer and devotee in the Vijay Vithala temple of Hampi, in the 15th-century Vijayanagara empire. You tap the glowing musical pillars on the beat to play an Indian orchestra, and each run opens a portal into a new dimension.

## Player fantasy

*What should the player feel like, or get to be?*

> The pillars sing when you touch them. You are the performer of an Indian orchestra in a temple.

## Why it's yours

*Where did this idea come from? What about it do you care about?*

> The temple has 56 pillars, with 7 pillars surrounding it for the 7 musical notes of India — that is where our team name SaReGaMa comes from. We are focusing on the musical pillars of Hampi.
