# Scope

You have the jam (11:00 AM to 4:00 PM). Decide now what's in, so cutting later is easy.

## In (must exist to call it a game)

*The smallest version someone can play start to finish.*

> Mode 1, the Rhythm Journey:
>
> - The Vijay Vithala temple, vertical scroll, the dancer devotee at the beat line.
> - 7 pillars on screen, one per note of the scale, scrolling down toward the line.
> - Tap the lit pillar's key on the beat: S R G M P D N.
> - Points, plus a combo that rewards consecutive hits.
> - 3 lives: a wrong tap costs a life, a missed beat scores nothing.
> - Perfect hits drop a collectible, collected automatically.
> - Portals: hit the beat enough and a portal opens into a new dimension; new instrumental pillars join and a different Indian instrument plays.
> - Title screen, score, game over, restart.
