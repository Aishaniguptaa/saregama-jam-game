## From the kit

> The game is set in the Vijay Vithala temple of Hampi, Vijayanagara empire, 15th century, focusing on its musical pillars: 56 pillars, with 7 pillars surrounding it for the 7 notes. The pillars glow and are tapped on the beat. Indian instruments: Tabla, Sitar, Flute, Dhol, Santoor.

## Camera

> Fixed side-on, vertical scroll, the dancer at the beat line. The mouse moves the camera as a look control only — a gentle sway across the temple, no effect on scoring.

## Note for M4 (Claude)

> "Realistic" and "grandeur" are the ambition, but Blender is not installed and there are no temple models in the kit, so the temple at M4 will be three.js primitives unless the makers choose to download licensed models. Options to put to them at M4: stone-styled primitives with canvas textures and strong lighting; or downloaded licensed temple models (costs hunting time and a licence per file). Their call.
